Problem statement

Official theme

To develop a system for construction material procurement, QC, storage and accounts which can be sustainable with minimum supervision from the project manager.

As the project manager for this project, you just hired a new store manager but he lacks the experience required to handle large scale projects. The project starts in 15 days from today. For the efficient movement of materials in and out of the construction site, you are supposed to set up a material procurement system which can sustain with your minimum involvement once the project starts. The following conditions must be met through your system.

  1. Prepare the layout of the store keeping in mind the consumables, non-consumables, equipment, and safety gear.

  2. The construction site has 2 gates, based on the location of the store in the site, decide which gate will be used as ingress and for departure.

  3. All vehicles numbers must be recorded when they enter and exit the site. You are free to use security and technology available at your disposal to accomplish this.

  4. The materials vehicle driver must bring in the invoice. A copy must remain at the security, one with the store manager and one copy must go to the accounts section.

  5. The materials must pass certain quality checks, please ensure the QA/QC inspector is working with the store manager for the inspection of the materials. Keep in mind the proximity of the material testing lab with the store. If rejected, the material must leave the premises immediately. Proper paperwork must be maintained.

  6. Accepted material after quality check must go to their designated storage slot as defined during Site Layout planning.

Official wording from the course brief (Construction Workshop problem statements).

Project context

The mall is an 8-acre site with 7 interconnected blocks, 3 underground parking levels and about 12,000 m³ of concrete PS1. PS1 identified efficient labour, material and vehicle circulation on a constrained site as the main site-management challenge. The PS1 visit to Rokhade Castle showed what happens without it: materials kept ~10 m away for lack of space and no dedicated pedestrian or vehicle routes PS1 observation.

7.4 Gate and vehicle control

Gate selection

The ingress gate is the one closest to the store and lab on the PS1 layout, with the departure gate chosen so loaded and empty trucks do not cross pedestrian or visitor areas. [Confirm gates on the PS1 drawing]

Vehicle controls

  • Delivery appointment
  • Vehicle registration and driver induction; PPE
  • Entry / exit register (vehicle no., time in / out)
  • Weighment or quantity check where applicable
  • Unloading zone with banksman / spotter; reversing control
  • Traffic segregation; no queuing on the public road

7.5 Quality-control plan

  • Inspection and Test Plan (ITP) per material, with hold and witness points
  • Checklists, test registers, calibration records and traceability to lot numbers
  • Release authority: QA/QC engineer
  • Concrete: approved mix design, batch tickets, slump / workability, temperature where required, cube sampling and testing, pour records

Limitations

  • Store and gate positions depend on the PS1 layout drawing.
  • No vendor, rate or quantity is stated; all registers are templates.
  • Test frequencies are left to the specification and ITP.

Key findings and learning outcomes

  • A material system is a chain of documents: each hand-over needs a signed record.
  • Inspection must happen before a GRN, not after.
  • The three invoice copies only control anything if they are reconciled.
  • Minimum supervision comes from clear SOPs, limits and an exception list — not from fewer checks.

Conclusion

The proposed system moves every material from indent to payment through one traceable chain, with joint QA/QC and store inspection before any GRN and rejected material leaving under its own paperwork. The three invoice copies give security, store and accounts each a record to reconcile, and the project manager sees only exceptions. Store and gate positions, quantities and vendors are confirmed from the PS1 drawing, BOQ and real quotations during the 15-day set-up.
