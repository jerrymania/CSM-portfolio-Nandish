Phased site layout — learning outcome

The team's implementation plan (slides 30–31) builds the mall in three phases. Hatched blocks are those under construction in that phase; completed blocks are re-used for staging, offices and labour amenities. Proposed schematic — not to scale.

PhaseUnder constructionGate 2 (north)Gate 1 (south)Support facilities
1Blocks 1, 3Heavy vehicles & material logisticsStaff, visitors & labour accessBlock 1 staging & storage; site office & testing lab near Block 3; labour accommodation & amenities near Gate 1; temporary Phase-1 safety barricade
2Blocks 4, 5Heavy vehicles & material logisticsStaff, visitors & labour accessBlock 1 → staging & storage; Block 3 → site office & testing lab; labour accommodation near Gate 1; barricade moved south
3Blocks 2, 6, 7Staff, visitors & labour accessHeavy vehicles & material logisticsBlock 1 → labour accommodation & amenities; Block 5 → site office & testing lab; Block 4 → staging & storage

What the phasing achieves

  • Material traffic enters on the side away from finished, occupied areas
  • Gate roles swap in Phase 3 so heavy vehicles reach the southern blocks directly
  • Completed blocks host staging, offices and amenities, so the open area stays free for work
  • A temporary barricade separates live work from finished blocks

illustrative interpretation of the drawings

Read with care

  • 'CP' is marked near each gate but is not defined on the slide
  • Use of completed blocks for labour accommodation needs safety and statutory checking
  • The drawings are schematic and not to scale

Review notes — the deck's figures are kept as presented

  • Admixture counted twice: line 1 (₹5,05,66,800) already contains ₹38,76,000 of admixture from slide 35, and line 2 adds ₹10,20,000 more.
  • Plant purchase vs resale: the ₹10.50 lakh resale is deducted, but the ₹15 lakh purchase is not a listed cost line unless 'installation' (also ₹15 lakh) is meant to be the purchase — confirm.
  • Mix proportions: the M35 ratio is printed as '01:0.5.01' and its solids total 1,000 kg/m³ (400 + 200 + 400), far below a normal concrete mass; nominal ratios are not approved mix designs.
  • Scope: RMC rates include supply, transport and the supplier's QC; the own-plant list should be checked for like-for-like scope (transit mixers, pumping, testing) before a saving is claimed.

Conclusion

PS1 sets the base for every later sheet: an 8-acre, seven-block mall with three parking levels and about 12,000 m³ of concrete. Two site visits contrasted a cramped vertical site with a documented, RERA-approved commercial project; the team listed the RERA and local-body documents, the labour-welfare provisions, and a three-phase layout that swaps gate roles as work moves south. The deck's cost study favours an own second-hand batching plant (₹5.84 Cr effective vs ₹6.10 Cr RMC, ≈ ₹25 lakh saving) over 468 working days — a result to confirm once the review notes are resolved.
