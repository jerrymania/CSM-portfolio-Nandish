Problem statement

Official theme

To develop a surveying plan for a particular site and a system to manage in-house engineers and outsourced work.

The project execution will start in 5 days. The survey work is outsourced to a Bengaluru-based surveying consultant. Their team equipped with the total station will be traveling to Hubballi for the permanent benchmark location and marking purpose. Using the site layout plan, mark the important benchmark locations throughout the site and mark the RLs when the surveying team arrives. Please keep in mind the surveying team charges Rs.10,000 plus travel and stay expenses every time they come down to the site. Plan your surveying work accordingly to minimize the expenses.

Furthermore, if your site has qualified civil engineers, why did your company outsource the surveying work. Brainstorm the possible reasons.

Project context

Seven interconnected blocks and three underground parking levels on 8 acres PS1 mean every block, pathway and basement must sit on one control network. An error in one block's grid carries into the connecting pathways and the next block. No site coordinates, dimensions, RLs, grid spacing or benchmark values are given in the source material, so none are stated here.

Visit 1 — consolidated scope (proposed)

  • Boundary verification
  • Primary control and all permanent BMs
  • Datum transfer and block grid reference lines
  • Excavation limits for basements
  • Independent check and signed handover

Between visits — in-house engineer

  • Level transfer from BMs to work fronts
  • Offsets and routine setting-out from handed-over control
  • Daily check shots; BM inspection
  • Request a visit only at a milestone or if a BM is disturbed

7.4 Why outsource — brainstorm

Equipment

Calibrated total station and software without buying or maintaining them.

Specialist skill

Daily practice in traversing and coordinate work.

Independent check

A third party verifies the control the whole project depends on.

Workload

Site engineers are busiest at mobilisation.

Liability

Signed survey records from a responsible firm.

Speed

An experienced crew sets the network faster.

Limitations

  • No coordinates, RLs or point positions are given; all are for the surveyor to establish.
  • The schematic is conceptual; real positions follow the approved layout and excavation limits.
  • Tolerances come from the project specification.

Key findings and learning outcomes

  • Survey control is the reference for every later trade; mistakes compound across interconnected blocks.
  • Visit cost is controlled by preparation, not by cutting checks.
  • Outsourcing the primary control and keeping routine work in-house balances cost, skill and independence.

Conclusion

A consolidated scope lets the Bengaluru consultant set the whole primary control and benchmark network in one visit, with later visits only at milestones or after disturbance. Benchmarks sit outside excavation and traffic zones with redundancy and closed checks, and a hold point stops excavation until the control is independently checked. In-house engineers then carry routine setting-out from the handed-over network.
