Problem statement

Official theme

To determine optimum labour force through the various stages of construction.

The concrete required for each Block of this project can be obtained from the Quantity take-off. Based on the given numbers, Figure out the following –

Number of skilled labors (Fabricators, carpenters, masons)

Number of unskilled labors (usually called as the concrete gang)

Keep the following points in mind

  1. There will be a peak time during execution where a large number of labor is required and then the slack time when the smaller number will suffice.

  2. If you hire less labor, it will impact your productivity

  3. If you hire more labor, you won't have enough work to engage them and they will leave.

  4. You should have all the prevailing wages in your area and the minimum wage as specified by the government.

  5. Your company must sign a contract with the labor supplier, you should know all the legal and technical details of such a contract.

Official wording from the course brief (Construction Workshop problem statements).

Wage-rate source note

Rates are left as “to be verified from current notification”. A Karnataka consolidated minimum-wage notification dated 22 May 2026 is reported, and has been challenged in the High Court. Use only the official gazette text, with zone, category, basic, VDA and effective date verify.

Labour contract — points for legal review

  • Labour-only vs item-rate / subcontract package
  • Scope, measurable outputs, measurement and payment cycle
  • Attendance, muster roll, wage register; payment by bank
  • Wages ≥ applicable minimum incl. VDA
  • Statutory compliance (registrations, contributions, BOCW cess as applicable)
  • Safety, PPE, accommodation and welfare
  • Insurance (employee compensation)
  • Dispute resolution and termination

Peak / slack management

  • Mobilise trades just ahead of their window; demobilise as a stage closes
  • Stagger blocks so not all are at peak together
  • Redeploy gangs between blocks instead of hiring
  • Limit simultaneous fronts on the constrained site
  • Coordinate with material deliveries, pours, access and safety

Risks, limitations and mitigation

Too many workers in slackStaggered blocks; redeployment
UnderpaymentBasic + VDA check before each payroll
Congestion on siteFront limits; staggered shifts
AccidentsInduction, PPE, toolbox talks
AbsenteeismWelfare, timely wages, attendance tracking

Limitations

  • No block-wise quantities, productivity records or headcounts are available.
  • Worked example and histogram are illustrative.
  • Wage rates must be verified from the applicable notification.

Key findings and learning outcomes

  • Labour numbers follow from quantities and productivity, not from guesswork.
  • Productivity units (per worker vs per gang) must be stated or the numbers mislead.
  • Wage compliance means basic plus VDA for the right zone and date.

Conclusion

The optimum labour force is computed trade by trade from the block-wise take-off: man-days from quantity and productivity, crew size from available days, then a histogram to smooth peak and slack. Until the take-off and programme are supplied the page provides the method, templates and a clearly illustrative example, with wages tied to the verified notification and a contract checklist for legal review.
